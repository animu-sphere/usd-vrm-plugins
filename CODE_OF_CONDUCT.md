# Code of Conduct

We want `usd-vrm-plugins` to be a welcoming project for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Please:

- be respectful and constructive;
- assume good intent, while listening when someone says an interaction caused
  harm;
- focus criticism on the code, documentation, or proposal rather than on a
  person; and
- help keep discussions useful, accessible, and on topic.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and deliberately
disruptive conduct.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive. Decisions
will be made with care and explained when it is practical to do so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when it involves a safety or privacy concern. For
conduct concerns that are not security issues, open a private message to the
repository maintainers on GitHub.

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project spaces.
